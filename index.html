Nine years across live production, post-production, creative agencies, and enterprise entertainment. Most IT people don't get creative work, and most creatives don't get enterprise tech. I've lived in both. Flip the cards.

Live & unforgiving

Media ops and cinematography.

Flip ↻

Flip ↻

Built and ran the entire IT function for a creative studio trusted with pre-release content from major film studios: security, networks, storage, identity, budgets, and a TPN-aligned remote platform for hundreds of artists.

Multi-region entertainment ops.

Running IT and AV operations across a multi-region enterprise environment in entertainment, where technology decisions touch thousands of people. Same obsession as day one, new tools: AI opened a whole new layer of what I can build.
